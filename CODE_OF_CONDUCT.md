# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community. Contributors should
be constructive, assume good intent, respect differing experience and viewpoints, and focus
feedback on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently exclude
participants whose conduct is harmful to the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[@ezdeployhq](https://github.com/ezdeployhq). Maintainers will handle reports
confidentially and avoid conflicts of interest when assigning a reviewer.
